---
title: Vegan Chocolate Lava Cake
published: true
servings: 6
ingredients:
  - name: Dark baking chocolate
    variable: 150
    unit: gr
  - name: Vegetable margarine
    variable: 70
    unit: gr
  - name: Plant-based milk
    variable: 140
    unit: ml
  - name: Powdered sugar
    variable: 70
    unit: gr
  - name: Flour
    variable: 50
    unit: gr
---

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
2. Melt the chocolate, margarine and plant-based milk in the microwave or over a double boiler, stirring regularly until smooth and homogeneous. Be careful not to burn the mixture.
3. Add the powdered sugar and flour to the melted mixture, stir well.
4. Distribute the batter into greased muffin molds. Optional: insert a chocolate square in the center of each mold for an even more flowing center.
5. Bake for 9 minutes (timing may vary depending on the oven).
6. Let rest 5 to 10 minutes before unmolding to prevent the cakes from breaking.
7. Serve warm or at room temperature.
